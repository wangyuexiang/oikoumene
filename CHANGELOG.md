# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-08

First public release.

- Old World extent (30°W–150°E, 0–80°N) on the Equal Earth projection
- 29 border slices, 500 BCE → 2000 CE, from historical-basemaps, with ghosted previous slice
- Shaded relief, rivers, lakes, ranges, deserts, peaks from Natural Earth / basemap-data
- 128 cities with multilingual name genealogies; 29 with taglines and histories
- 62 people with biographies and life traces
- 138 events in six categories; chronicle column; era timeline; tour mode
- Search across cities, people, polities, events
- UI in English, Chinese, French, German, Spanish
- Deep links: `?year=`, `?lang=`, `?journey=`, `?tour=`
