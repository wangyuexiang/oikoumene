# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-08

- Civilization lens (*Focus on* in the phase card, `?civ=`) and category solo by double-click

- Theme focus mode: four stories (Alexander and Greek thought, Barbarian Migrations, Mongol Invasions, Rise of Islam), `?theme=&stop=` deep links
- Population and GDP overlays on the timeline (world, Europe, China, Middle East)
- People layer with 62 biographies and life traces; city histories; Wikipedia buttons
- Six UI languages (Italian added); English-first README with FR/DE/IT translations
- Marker click fix; clearer category chips; Themes menu separated from layers

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-08

First public release.

- Old World extent (30°W–150°E, 0–80°N) on the Equal Earth projection
- 29 border slices, 500 BCE → 2000 CE, from historical-basemaps, with ghosted previous slice
- Shaded relief, rivers, lakes, ranges, deserts, peaks from Natural Earth / basemap-data
- 128 cities with multilingual name genealogies; 29 with taglines and histories
- 62 people with biographies and life traces
- 138 events in six categories; chronicle column; era timeline; tour mode
- Search across cities, people, polities, events
- UI in English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Chinese
- Deep links: `?year=`, `?lang=`, `?journey=`, `?tour=`
