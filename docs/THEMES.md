# Themes — proposed focus stories

<sub>[Français](THEMES.fr.md) · [Deutsch](THEMES.de.md) · [Italiano](THEMES.it.md) · [中文](THEMES.zh.md)</sub>

A **theme** is a story you step into: everything not part of it fades, the timeline shows its span, and you walk it stop by stop with a card at each. Four are live — Alexander and Greek thought, the Great Migrations, the Mongol Invasions, the Rise of Islam — and the twenty below are the ones we think are worth building next.

**Vote**: each has an issue tagged [`theme`](https://github.com/wangyuexiang/oikoumene/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Atheme). React 👍 on the ones you want; we build in vote order, roughly one a week. **Missing one?** Open an issue with the [theme proposal template](https://github.com/wangyuexiang/oikoumene/issues/new?template=theme.md) — a title, the years and a one-paragraph pitch is enough.

## How the twenty were chosen

Five tests, applied to about forty candidates:

1. **It crosses the map.** A theme should use the fact that this atlas runs from the Atlantic to Japan. Stories that stay inside one modern country were set aside, however good.
2. **Every stop can be dated and placed.** Routes and diffusions with documented stops first; stories that need hand-drawn borders (Islam's twenty-year frontiers, the Ottoman advance) come later, once the border pipeline exists.
3. **Not only wars.** Three and a half of the first four themes are military. The twenty are balanced: eight on ideas, religion, learning and art; five on trade and travel; four on disease and technology; three on empire.
4. **Someone will share it.** Each has a hook that works in one sentence — "Europe was saved by a funeral", "coffee reached Vienna in a Turkish baggage train".
5. **It talks to the others.** Xuanzang starts where the Mongol theme ends in China; the Black Death starts where the Mongols left off at Caffa; paper leaves Talas (last stop of the Islam theme) and arrives at Gutenberg. The twenty form a web, not a list.

Dropped, and why: Hannibal (fits inside *Rome, rise and fall*), Goethe's Italian journey (fits inside *the Grand Tour*), the Enlightenment and the universities (fold into *the Republic of Letters* and *the translation road*), gunpowder and chess (fun, thin), Darwin and the Age of Discovery (leave the map), the Pax Mongolica (covered by *the Mongol Invasions*). The Silk Road is proposed as a permanent toggle layer, not a story.

## How a theme works

1. **Enter**: from the *Themes* menu, a search result, or `?theme=<id>`.
2. **Focus**: polities not part of the story fade; the timeline shows the theme's span with a dot per stop; the chronicle lists only its stops; the theme card gives title, span, summary and sources.
3. **Walk**: ← → or the chronicle moves stop to stop; each stop flies the camera and opens its card.
4. **Leave**: *Exit theme* or Esc restores the full map at the year you left.

Data for a theme is one JSON file in `public/data/themes/<id>.json`, listed in `themes/index.json`: `{id, kind, tags, span, zoom, title:{en,zh}, subtitle, summary, source, polities:[regex…], stops:[{y, c:[lng,lat], name:{en,zh}, text:{en,zh}, w}]}`. `polities` names the borders that stay bright in focus mode. Deep link: `?theme=<id>&stop=<n>`.

**Not planned**: city emblems (128 bespoke glyphs is a design project of its own); themes outside the map's extent; anything that needs a backend.
